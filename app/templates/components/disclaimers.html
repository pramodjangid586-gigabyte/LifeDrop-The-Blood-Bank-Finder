Emergency Information & Coordination Notice

BloodConnect is an information and coordination platform. We do NOT supply blood directly, nor do we guarantee real-time stock availability, donor attendance, or medical suitability. In acute emergencies, contact the treating hospital or the nearest authorized blood bank immediately.

"Eligibility must be confirmed according to applicable blood-donation guidelines." Official pre-donation health evaluations (hemoglobin, intervals, infection screening) must be conducted at a licensed collection center.
